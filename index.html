How your Sales Director should use this

Simple operating procedure and limitations for territory checks.

1. Look up the requested ZIPs

Use Sales Check, paste all ZIPs and choose the campaign type. ZIPs missing from suppression still need validation—not an automatic “available” label.

2. Check client conflict evidence

Red locks appear only for manually verified active exclusive assignments. Non-exclusive overlap is allowed under your policy. List-ID and city matches are informational flags.

3. Evaluate actual dialing activity

Use mean attempts per record, 5+ attempts percentage, last call date, and recent call volume. Heavy use can prompt rest, new data procurement or an Operations review. None measures actual reachable households.

4. Confirm and document territory

Operations confirms ZIP-level scope, campaign type, exclusivity and status; then records the decision in Territory Manager. Export the assignments CSV for teammates and backups.

5. Updating this offline-managed version

This HTML contains a data snapshot and is not live-synced. Local territory assignments persist only in the browser that saved them. Export/import the CSV whenever transferring to another computer or opening a refreshed HTML.

6. Data interpretation safeguards

NEW/zero-call in a suppression export does not mean legally callable. Raw records and status are not a full inventory of permissioned leads. No direct homeowner data is included in this dashboard.
